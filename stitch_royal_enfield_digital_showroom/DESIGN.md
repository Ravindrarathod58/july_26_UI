## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the bespoke mechanical artistry, storied provenance, and rugged prestige of classic handcrafted British and European motorcycling culture. The digital atmosphere mimics an intimate private workshop late at night—forged steel, aged patinas, turned brass petcocks, hand-stitched bridle leather saddles, and high-performance engineering specs.

The visual direction merges **Industrial Elegance with Tactile Heritage**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, mechanical, contemplative, enduring, uncompromisingly premium.
- **Audience:** Discerning enthusiasts, mechanical purists, collectors, and riders seeking authenticity, visceral machinery, and modern reliability wrapped in timeless coachbuilding traditions.
- **Aesthetic Principles:**
  - *Heirloom Precision:* Elements mimic machined metal plates, etched plaques, knurled dials, and chassis stampings.
  - *No Plastic, Pure Materiality:* Generous dark negative space, micro-textures, hairline metallic dividers, and warm warm-cast lighting.
  - *Editorial Curation:* Full-bleed cinematic photography balanced by precise, architectural data layouts that treat technical specifications as high art.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive, low-key lighting environment anchored by cast iron, hot-rolled steel, and burnished vintage brass.

- **Primary (`#C87D32` - Aged Vintage Copper):** Evokes copper rivets, exhaust heat-wrap tint, and burnished metals. Utilized for primary interactive triggers, active highlights, key focal rules, and insignia outlines.
- **Secondary (`#D99B52` - Brushed Brass):** Lighter, polished gold-brass used for subtle secondary highlights, badge typography, mechanical counters, and hover micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#1E2E24` - British Racing Green):** A deep, subterranean forest tone. Reserved for contextual utility badges, subtle surface tints, heritage seals, and specialized technical indicators.
- **Neutral Core (`#121314` - Dark Charcoal / Matte Black):** The canvas base mimicking raw, matte-treated powder-coated frames and dark machine bays.
  - Canvas Base: `#0D0E0F`
  - Structural Surface Container: `#121314`
  - Elevated Component Surface: `#1A1C1E`
  - Subtle Overlay / Border: `#2C2E30`
- **Typography & Content Tone (`#F4EFE6` - Raw Parchment):** High-legibility warm off-white, removing the stark clinical glare of pure white while preserving crisp editorial clarity against deep chassis tones. Muted text steps down to `#A69E92` (weathered stone) and `#6B665E` (machined alloy).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes two distinct eras of craft: the romantic, literary editorial authority of handcrafted coachbuilders alongside the clinical, stamped monospaced precision of technical workshop blueprints.

- **Headlines (Newsreader):** Brings warmth, classical proportion, and heritage drama. Large displays feature editorial styling, conveying custom fabrication history, bespoke models, and storytelling.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Source Sans 3):** Clean, neutral, humanist, and utilitarian. Ensures effortless scanability without competing with the ornate character of the serif headlines.
- **Labels, Metrics & Engineering Specs (Space Mono):** Used in all-caps for chassis numbers, torque curves, engine displacements, toggle buttons, and metallurgical notes. Communicates cold-forged precision and workshop registry aesthetics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced, deliberate structural rhythm that emphasizes cinematic negative space and architectural containment.

- **Grid Structure:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `margin-desktop` (64px) and `gutter-lg` (40px). Max content container constrained to 1440px to retain intimate editorial balance.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `margin` (32px) and `gutter` (24px).
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile` (20px) and `gutter-sm` (16px).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace Philosophy:** Spacing follows an 8-point system. Component padding leverages generous vertical margins (`space-xl` / 48px) to allow mechanical diagrams and motorcycle profiles room to breathe.
- **Section Breaks & Division:** Utilize etched mechanical hairlines (`1px solid #2C2E30`) capped with miniature diamond registration notches or monospaced reference labels (`SECTION 04 // SPEC`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews floating, synthetic Drop Shadows in favor of **Tonal Surface Layering** and **Machined Metallic Edges**:

1. **Base Layer (`#0D0E0F`):** Deep chassis ground for entire screens and full-width stage wrappers.
2. **Surface Tier (`#121314`):** Cards, specification tables, and structural compartments. Outlined with subtle, razor-thin borders (`1px solid #232528`).
3. **Elevated Elements & Modals (`#1A1C1E`):** Distinct sub-assemblies and popovers. Employs a crisp, low-intensity ambient cast shadow: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 0 1px rgba(200, 125, 50, 0.15)`. The subtle perimeter copper-tint halo mimics light catching a polished bevel.
4. **Tactile Insets:** Form fields, readouts, and engineering displays use an inner inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`) and a darker backing (`#0A0B0B`) to look counter-sunk into the instrument cluster.

## Shapes

The shape system strictly uses **Sharp Geometry (`0` / 0px radius)** to preserve an authentic, machined-sheet-metal and billet-aluminum character:

- **Rectilinear Discipline:** Primary buttons, cards, tags, and inputs maintain 90-degree squared corners, evoking instrument dials, cast iron blocks, and VIN stamp plates.
- **Chamfered & Beveled Accents:** For high-visibility hero callouts and mechanical badges, 45-degree corner notches (3px–6px clipped corners via CSS clip-path) are permitted to emulate tool-and-die stamped metal plates.
- **Hairline Framing:** Micro-borders (1px) establish structural discipline across dense specs and imagery without visual bulk.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button (Machined Brass):** Squared edges, solid `#C87D32` background with `#0D0E0F` text set in `label-md` (Space Mono, all-caps). On hover: shifts to `#D99B52` with an ambient copper underglow. Active state features a 1px inset depression.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button (Billet Frame):** Transparent background, 1px border of `#6B665E`, `#F4EFE6` text. On hover: border transforms to `#C87D32` with a subtle copper-tinted surface tint (`rgba(200, 125, 50, 0.08)`).
- **Destructive / Halt:** Matte dark charcoal with a thin crimson registration line and muted ochre label text.

### Chips & Data Badges
- **Engineering Tag:** Monospaced (`label-sm`), all-caps text wrapped in `#1A1C1E` backing with a 1px `#2C2E30` border.
- **Heritage Badge:** Deep British Racing Green (`#1E2E24`) background with `#D99B52` (brushed brass) text and border, evoking cast enamel tank emblems.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Sunken `#0A0B0B` background, 1px `#2C2E30` border, `Source Sans 3` body input, and floating `Space Mono` uppercase labels. Focused state shifts border to 1px `#C87D32` with zero blur spread.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Precision-machined 14px square boxes (0px radius). Checked state displays a solid inner copper brass plate (`#C87D32`) centered within a dark recess.

### Cards & Spec Panels
- **Vehicle Showcase Card:** Unbroken 1px border (`#232528`), `#121314` fill, rich photography top-half framed by a hairline divider. Bottom half houses the model name in `headline-sm` (`Newsreader`), followed by a two-column technical data table in `Space Mono`.

### Specialty Components
- **Chassis Spec Sheet (Data Grid):** Alternating rows in subtle tone variations (`#121314` and `#161819`) separated by dotted lines. Left-aligned engineering labels in `#A69E92`, right-aligned values in `#F4EFE6` brass-accented font.
- **Inspection Plaque:** A metallic-styled hero banner framing limited-edition serial runs, complete with simulated twin screw rivets (`+` crosshairs) placed in the upper-left and upper-right corners.
